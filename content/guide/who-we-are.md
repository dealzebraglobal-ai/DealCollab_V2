# Who We Are

**Last updated: [17/05/2026]**

## The company behind DealCollab

DealCollab is the flagship platform built by **DealZebra Global Intelligence
LLP** — a technology company building next-generation infrastructure for M&A
and investment banking.

We started from a simple conviction: the M&A industry deserves better
infrastructure. Transactions still run on fragmented information, manual
outreach, and relationship-bound discovery. The industry has advanced
financially; the systems underneath it have not kept pace. DealCollab is our
answer to that gap — an intelligence layer that structures how capital
connects with opportunity.

## Who builds it

DealZebra is a small, cross-skilled team from **investment banking,
technology, mathematics, and engineering**. That mix is deliberate: it lets us
approach a transaction from three angles at once — financial, analytical, and
technical — rather than bolting technology onto a banker's workflow or
finance onto an engineer's product.

We are experimental by disposition. We question existing processes, study the
real friction inside live transactions, and build systems that bring
structure, clarity, and intelligence to how deals actually happen.

## What we're building

DealCollab is one part of a broader thesis: that modern dealmaking should be
faster, clearer, and more connected than the legacy model allows. Our focus
across everything we build is the same — technology that makes deal
discovery, collaboration, and execution more efficient for M&A
professionals, while keeping confidentiality and control with the people who
own the information.

We approach this as infrastructure, not a directory: matching driven by
structured intelligence rather than volume, confidentiality held by design,
and a platform meant to earn the trust of the advisors and principals who use
it.

## Founder's note

Having worked in both engineering and investment banking, one thing became
clear: the M&A industry has immense intellectual depth, but the
infrastructure supporting it is still evolving. DealZebra was started with a
straightforward intention — to build thoughtful technology that supports
dealmakers and removes the friction that sits inside transactions today. If
we can make deals clearer, faster, and more collaborative, that unlocks real
value for entrepreneurs, advisors, and capital alike.
